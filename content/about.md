I build things for the web and app — usually the design and the code both.

Most of my work sits in the space between the two. I like the part where a
rough idea becomes a layout, then a prototype, then something people actually
use. Design decisions that never survive implementation aren't really
decisions, so I'd rather hold both ends of the problem at once.
